About Lagos

Lagos is Nigeria's largest city and its main commercial centre, with a metropolitan population commonly estimated at more than 15 million people. It is home to people from every part of Nigeria and has a strong Yoruba heritage. English is the official language, and Yoruba is widely spoken. Set between the Atlantic coast and a busy lagoon, Lagos is known for its markets, music, art, food and beaches, which makes it a great place to do business and to visit.
