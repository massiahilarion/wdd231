1. Site Name

Discover Akwa Ibom

The name is short, easy to remember, and includes the state name, which helps people find the site when they search for Akwa Ibom. The word “Discover” tells visitors the site is about finding new places, food, and experiences.

2. Site Purpose

Discover Akwa Ibom is a three-page information website that helps tourists, diaspora Nigerians planning a visit, and local residents find things to see and do in Akwa Ibom State. It brings attractions, local food and culture, festivals and events, and practical travel tips together in one organized place.

Information and services provided

  • Attractions: a filterable list of places to visit.
  • Food and culture: local dishes and cultural highlights.
  • Festivals and events: what is happening and when.
  • Travel tips: practical advice for planning a trip.
  • Current weather: live conditions from a weather API.
  • Inquiry form: a way to ask questions or get in touch.

3. Scenarios

These are questions a typical visitor would ask. The website content is designed to answer them.

Scenario 1: The diaspora traveler

Who: A Nigerian living abroad who is planning a trip home in December.

Questions: What are the best places to visit in Akwa Ibom? What will the weather be like when I arrive? Which festivals or events will be happening during my stay?

Answered by: the Home page weather, the Attractions page, and the festivals section of Plan Your Visit.

Scenario 2: The first-time tourist

Who: A visitor from another country who has never been to the state.

Questions: What local food should I try? What cultural experiences are worth my time? What do I need to know before I travel, and how can I ask someone a question?

Answered by: the food and culture content, the travel tips, and the inquiry form.

Scenario 3: The local resident

Who: Someone who lives in the state and wants something to do with family or friends.

Questions: What events are coming up? Which attractions are near me that I have not visited yet?

Answered by: the festivals and events section and the filterable attractions list.

  • Palm Gold #E0A526
  • Warm Cream #FFF8E7

  • Charcoal #222222
Where each color will be used

Forest GreenHeadings, header and footer backgrounds, links, main buttons
Palm GoldAccents: borders, underlines, and link hover states
Warm CreamMain page background
WhiteCard and table backgrounds, text on green backgrounds

Dark text on the cream and white backgrounds, and white text on forest green, keep contrast high for accessibility.
